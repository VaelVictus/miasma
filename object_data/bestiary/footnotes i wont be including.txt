See my Work’s Account of My First Trip Amongst The Whale-Bone’s of Ring-in-Opal, as told to me by their emissary, the Young-Whale-Bone, With the Beard of Piss, Handsome and Other.

(2) He still will not let me near if he can help it, he seems to have the ear of the Mayor. They spend so much time hand in hand, almost walking paths in their discussions about me, their theological debates about my provenance. If I am breaking some taboo by loving her, then at least our love ripples outwards and makes some enchantments in this dead place! There is some horrid, possessive jealousy at work in him, and he has infected all the others against me, indeed almost everyone has met with me has been wont to bully. I can see their black and brown bruises, and I cannot think who else could abuse such them so often. Mine Fionn keeps defying him, however, when nobody is watching, prising at my armour, walking between them, basking in their shade, and pulling the room in my neck again. She still, after everything has not shown any interest in the Diplomat, but I am happy nonetheless. She says that she does not like the colour, and that it makes her legs feel funny when she looks at it.
